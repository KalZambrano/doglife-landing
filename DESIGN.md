## Brand & Style
The design system embodies an artisanal, organic pet-care boutique rooted in wholesome canine nutrition and single-ingredient purity. Designed specifically around natural dehydrated chicken paws snacks (*patitas deshidratadas para perros*), the aesthetic merges slow-crafted warmth with modern Peruvian pet boutique refinement.

The visual style blends **Soft Tactile Warmth** and **Editorial Minimalism**:
- **Aesthetic Tone:** Earthy, welcoming, trustworthy, and premium. It avoids clinical sterility and chaotic cartoonishness in favor of sun-dried hues, balanced culinary-grade presentation, and delicate artisanal detailing.
- **Micro-Interactions & Motifs:** Soft, spring-damped tactile elevations upon hover; subtle debossed badge borders; pill-shaped dietary badges; and subtle curved organic contours echoing natural paw shapes without visual clutter.

## Colors
The palette evokes gently slow-dehydrated proteins, golden marrow, toasted grains, and unbleached cotton paper.

- **Primary (`#6B4423` - Toasted Mocha):** Anchors primary brand actions, crucial headline typography, and focal navigational items. Communicates heritage and hearty organic substance.
- **Secondary (`#D4A359` - Honey Nectar):** Serves as an accent for nutritional claims, artisan seals, high-value highlights, and interactive active states.
- **Tertiary (`#A77B52` - Warm Caramel):** Used for secondary controls, supporting metadata, ingredient notes, and muted borders.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#F7F3EB` - Cream Linen):** Primary background base, accompanied by `#FCFBF7` (Soft Eggshell) for elevated cards and `#FFFDF9` (Warm Milk) for interactive input wells.
- **Functional Accents:** `#4A6B35` (Herbal Sage) for 100% natural and single-ingredient certifications; `#93382D` (Earthy Clay) reserved exclusively for critical warnings and out-of-stock indicators.

## Typography
The typographic hierarchy pairs the editorial grace of **Playfair Display** with the friendly, ergonomic clarity of **Plus Jakarta Sans**.

- **Headlines & Display:** Playfair Display introduces bespoke culinary craftsmanship, making packaging headlines, product origins, and benefit titles feel curated and trustworthy.
- **Body & Captions:** Plus Jakarta Sans provides high-legibility geometry with gentle humanist terminals, ensuring feeding instructions, nutritional statistics, and checkout forms are instantly scannable.
- **Editorial Flourishes:** Italicized sub-phrases in Playfair Display (e.g., *100% natural snack deshidratado*) provide artisanal rhythm in marketing banners.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout adheres to a 12-column fluid grid on desktop (max container width: 1280px) and collapses into a responsive 4-column system for mobile devices.

- **Desktop (>= 1024px):** 12 columns, 1.5rem (`24px`) gutters, and 3rem (`48px`) margins to create breathing room around product imagery and ingredient breakdowns.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8 columns, 1.25rem (`20px`) gutters, and 2rem (`32px`) margins.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, 1rem (`16px`) gutters, and 1.25rem (`20px`) outer canvas margins. Sticky bottom bar for direct cart action.
- **Rhythm:** Vertical layout sections use generous multiples of `space-xl` (`40px` to `80px`) to prevent visual crowding and maintain an unhurried, boutique atmosphere.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth avoids artificial, stark black drop-shadows. Instead, it relies on warm-tinted ambient diffusion and tonal layering reminiscent of natural sunlight over craft paper.

- **Surface Tiers:**
  - Base: `#F7F3EB` (Soft Cream canvas)
  - Layer 1 (Cards, Product Tiles): `#FCFBF7` with a fine `1px` inner-tone border (`#EADBCE`).
  - Layer 2 (Floating Modals, Flyout Cart, Dropdowns): `#FFFDF9` with warm ambient shadow: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(107, 68, 35, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(107, 68, 35, 0.04)`.
- **Borders & Outlines:** Always low-contrast earth tones (`#EADBCE` or `#DFC8B2`), never pure black or cool slate grey.
- **Tactile Transitions:** On hover, clickable surfaces rise by `2px` while ambient shadow shifts slightly warmer (`rgba(212, 163, 89, 0.14)`).

## Shapes
The shape system uses a balanced medium-rounded aesthetic (`roundedness: 2`), reflecting natural, organic forms:

- Standard structural panels, cards, and input fields inherit `0.5rem` (`8px`) corners.
- Interactive buttons, badge containers, and feature promo highlights use `rounded-lg` (`1rem` / `16px`) or fully rounded pill geometry (`9999px`) to emphasize a friendly, pet-safe warmth.
- Pill tags for product highlights (e.g., "Rico en Glucosamina", "100% Pollo Peruano") are fully rounded capsules.
